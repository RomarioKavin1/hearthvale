# Hearthvale — Demo Video Script (≤ 60 seconds)

**Recording setup:** screen-record the real Reddit post on r/hearthvale_dev (desktop for crispness; one mobile shot if easy). Use a FRESH world (I'll wipe before you record). No mic needed if you prefer captions — but VO reads warmer. OBS or QuickTime; 1080p; trim to <60s.

| Time | Shot | VO / Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Reddit feed scrolling; stop on the Hearthvale post — the floating-island splash with live stats. Click "Enter the Village". | "This is Hearthvale — a village your whole subreddit builds together." |
| 5–15s | Fresh world. The tour highlights tiles → tap → House rises. Tap beside it → build Wheat Field (card spotlit). | "Found your homestead anywhere you like — the game guides every step." |
| 15–25s | Field ripens (cut the wait), coin pip pops, tap → wheat banked; catch a golden sparkle → PERFECT double harvest. | "Everything grows in real time — tap the golden sparkle for a perfect harvest." |
| 25–35s | Market: sell wheat, price ticks, "the village needs planks" callout. | "Sell at the village market — prices move with what the village needs. Your wheat feeds someone else's windmill." |
| 35–45s | Village Hall sheet: contribute planks/bricks, bars fill, LEVEL UP → land ring expands with the celebration, castle grows. | "Together you raise the Village Hall — each level unlocks land, perks, and a bigger castle for everyone." |
| 45–55s | Quick montage: painting a roof, villagers strolling, another player's house appearing live, leaderboards, daily post in the feed. | "Villages grow one redditor at a time — every subreddit gets its own world." |
| 55–60s | Splash card again + app name. | "Hearthvale — build our village together. On Reddit, now." |

**Recording tips:** hide the harness/dev-panel (record the REAL Reddit post); do the Hall level-up with a second account or ask a friend to be villager #2 (population 2 requirement); keep the browser window wide so the map breathes.
